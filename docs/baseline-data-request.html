1. What we need

A baseline of customer complaints for missing items and wrong / mixed-up orders across all BBT branches, together with order volumes, so we can track the complaint rate over time and identify the branches most affected.

2. Definitions

TermMeaning
Missing itemCustomer reports one or more ordered items were absent from the order.
Wrong / mixed-upCustomer received a different order or the wrong item(s).
Complaint rateIn-scope complaints per 1,000 orders (the reporting metric).

3. Data required

DatasetFields
ComplaintsDate · business date · branch (name + code) · order number · channel / aggregator · category (missing / wrong / mixed-up) · item(s) affected · resolution & refund amount (if any).
Order volumeOrders per branch, per day, per channel — used as the denominator for the rate.

4. Period & granularity

ViewPeriodGranularity
TrendLast 12 monthsMonthly
Working baselineLast 12 weeksDaily
OngoingFrom go-live onwardDaily
Headline metric: missing + mixed-up complaints per 1,000 orders (12-week average), reported overall and per branch. Please include the raw counts alongside the rate.

5. Breakdowns & outputs

  • Branch ranking — all branches, highest complaint rate first.
  • By type — missing vs wrong/mixed-up.
  • By channel / aggregator — dine-in, pickup, each partner.
  • Top 20 items most reported as missing.
  • Monthly trend and daily series (counts + rate).
